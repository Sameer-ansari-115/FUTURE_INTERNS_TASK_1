# Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics Task 1

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing business sales data to understand sales performance, revenue trends, product demand, category performance, regional performance, and profitability.

The analysis was performed using Python and JupyterLab to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze revenue trends over time
- Identify top-selling products
- Identify high-value categories
- Identify high-value regions
- Analyze profitability by category and region
- Generate meaningful business insights
- Provide actionable recommendations for business growth

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- JupyterLab
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- CSV Dataset

---

## 📊 Dataset

The project uses a Superstore sales dataset containing business transaction information such as:

- Order Date
- Sales
- Profit
- Quantity
- Category
- Region
- Product Name
- Customer and order information

The dataset is stored inside the `data/` folder.

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Cleaning

The dataset was inspected and cleaned before performing the analysis. Missing values, data types, and important columns were checked.

### 2. Total Revenue Analysis

Total sales revenue was calculated to understand the overall business performance.

### 3. Monthly Revenue Trend

Monthly sales revenue was analyzed to identify changes in business performance over time.

### 4. Top-Selling Products

Products were analyzed based on quantity sold to identify the products with the highest demand.

### 5. High-Value Categories

Categories were compared based on total sales revenue to identify the categories generating the highest revenue.

### 6. High-Value Regions

Regions were analyzed based on total sales revenue to identify the strongest-performing geographical regions.

### 7. Profit Analysis

Profit was analyzed by category and region to understand which areas contribute most to business profitability.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- **Office Supplies** is the highest revenue-generating category, with approximately **719,047** in sales.
- **West** is the highest revenue-generating region, with approximately **725,458** in sales.
- **Technology** is the most profitable category, generating approximately **145,455** in profit.
- **November 2017** recorded the highest monthly revenue, with approximately **118,448** in sales.
- **Staples** is the top-selling product by quantity, with **215 units sold**.
- Revenue and profitability are not always driven by the same category, so both sales and profit should be considered when making business decisions.

---

## 📈 Business Recommendations

1. Maintain sufficient inventory for high-demand products such as Staples.
2. Focus marketing and customer retention strategies on high-performing regions such as the West.
3. Continue investing in the Technology category because of its strong profitability.
4. Plan promotional campaigns and inventory strategies around high-demand periods.
5. Review low-profit categories and evaluate pricing, discounts, and operating costs.
6. Use both revenue and profit metrics when evaluating business performance.
